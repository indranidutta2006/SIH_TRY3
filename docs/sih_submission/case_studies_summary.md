## 2. Key Executive Insights

1. **Conventional Diesel Fleet (Case A):** Low initial capex, but severe forward financial liability under escalating EU ETS carbon taxes ($80–$150/t) and FuelEU penalty multipliers.
2. **LNG Dual-Fuel Fleet (Case B):** Excellent near-term bridging technology (-24% WTW emissions), fully compliant through 2030, but faces methane slip compliance pressures post-2035.
3. **Methanol Transition Fleet (Case C):** Optimal capital payback (4.7 years) due to significantly lower engine retrofit capex ($6.5M vs $9.8M for LNG) and non-cryogenic ambient bunkering.
4. **Hydrogen Future Fleet (Case D):** Maximum decarbonization (-82% WTW emissions) using Capesize bulk carriers with renewable green electrolysis feedstock, achieving positive long-term ROI when carbon pricing exceeds $100/ton.
